# Contributing

Thanks for helping investigate KernelSU Next on MSM8998 / Maple.

## Ground rules
- Include exact source commit SHAs, device model, ROM build, and toolchain.
- Keep patches small and explain why each change is needed.
- Preserve upstream copyright notices and licenses.
- Do not submit proprietary blobs, private keys, credentials, or personal data.
- Do not claim support based only on compilation.
- Include logs and device test evidence for compatibility claims.
- Mark untested work as experimental.

## Patch review
Each patch should identify its upstream base commit, rationale, kernel-version assumptions, build/test results, and known limitations.
